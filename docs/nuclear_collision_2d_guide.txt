2D heavy-ion collision initial condition guide
==============================================

What this makes
---------------
This is the first heavy-ion collision prototype in this HIC folder. It is a
GPU toy model for visualizing how two incoming nuclei create an initial
transverse energy density and how that density evolves afterward.

The output HTML has three panels:

1. energy density evolution
   The produced matter after the two nuclei overlap.

2. incoming nuclear thickness
   The two moving nuclear thickness profiles before and during overlap.

3. eps2 / rms radius
   A simple eccentricity and size diagnostic over time.

Model
-----
The initial source uses a Glauber-like mixture:

T_A, T_B = moving Woods-Saxon-like transverse thickness profiles
wounded  = T_A * (1 - exp(-T_B)) + T_B * (1 - exp(-T_A))
binary   = T_A * T_B
e_source = norm * ((1 - alpha) * wounded + alpha * binary)

The evolution is a qualitative hydrodynamic-like toy model:

de/dt = diffusion * laplacian(e) - radial_expansion * div(e * r_hat) - damping * e

This is not a validated relativistic viscous hydrodynamics code yet. It is the
first step toward studying initial-condition evolution.

Useful parameters
-----------------
--grid-size
2D transverse grid resolution.

--impact-parameter
Offset between the two nuclei. Larger values make the initial energy density
more almond-shaped and increase eccentricity.

--mass-number
Approximate nuclear mass number.

--radius
Nuclear radius.

--skin-depth
Woods-Saxon surface diffuseness.

--binary-fraction
Mixing fraction between wounded-nucleon-like and binary-collision-like scaling.

--diffusion
Smoothing strength during evolution.

--radial-expansion
How strongly the produced energy density expands outward.

--damping
Decay rate of the energy density.

Example
-------
From the HIC directory:

..\gpu\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m hic.nuclear_collision_2d

More peripheral collision:

..\gpu\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m hic.nuclear_collision_2d --impact-parameter 10 --output outputs\nuclear_collision_b10.html

More central collision:

..\gpu\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m hic.nuclear_collision_2d --impact-parameter 1.5 --output outputs\nuclear_collision_b15.html
